About me

I am a PhD student in Biomedical Imaging Sciences at the University of Manchester, working on multimodal AI Agent for surgical understanding and assistance. My research interests include vision–language models, computer vision, and computer-assisted intervention.

Previously, I completed an MSc in Artificial Intelligence for Biomedicine and Healthcare at University College London (UCL), graduating with Distinction. My work has involved surgical instrument detection and pose estimation, medical image analysis, and machine learning.

I am interested in developing trustworthy AI systems that can understand complex clinical environments and support surgical workflows.

Research Projects

  • Multi-Instrument Detection and Pose Estimation in Endoscopic Surgery (2025). Developed the PitSurg dataset and MID-POSE, a model for surgical instrument detection and 2D pose estimation.
  • Quantitative Water–Fat Separation from Multi-Echo MRI (2025). Developed magnitude-only MRI estimators and used Rician maximum-likelihood fitting to improve parameter estimation.
  • Brain Microstructure Estimation from Diffusion MRI (2022–2023). Trained a self-supervised model for faster ball-and-stick parameter estimation from diffusion MRI signals.
